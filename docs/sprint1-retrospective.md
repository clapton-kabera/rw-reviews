# Sprint 1 Retrospective (Oct. 12th 2026 - Oct. 26th 2026)
## Sprint Goal
Deliver a working backend that allows users to create reviews and read reviews for a specific business.
* * *

## What Went Well?
- Seeded data was a good idea that made manual testing easy.
- The tests were able to catch errors and bugs earlier on.
- Communication between DevOps and Devs was timely and well coordinated.

## What Didn't Go Well?
- There was time lost figuring out why seeded data was leaking into the tests.
- The sprint felt a bit rushed since planning took more time than expected.

## What We Learned?
- We need to timebox meetings and phases to avoid time creep.
- Have separate profiles for test data and development data.

## What Shall We Do Better?
- Create timeboxes and handle meetings in a more timely manner.
